Processing parameters

Default distance settings

All distance values are entered in metres and converted to the analysis CRS map units.

SettingDefaultPurpose
Priority road buffer distance20 mCoverage classification and priority-buffer mask.
Secondary road buffer distance10 mLocal square buffers in Targeted Snap.
Maximum snapping distance25 mEndpoint snapping in Clip + Snap and Targeted Snap.
Midpoint densification interval10 mAnchor spacing in Clip + Snap.
Maximum centroid adjustment distance15 mValidated by the dialog but currently not used by the processing engine.

Output formats

  • GeoPackage (.gpkg): recommended; full field names and multiple layers.
  • Shapefile (.shp): compatible, but field names are limited to 10 characters.
  • File Geodatabase (.gdb): depends on a create-capable GDAL driver in the active QGIS installation.

Technical considerations

  • Road inputs must be line layers and the study boundary must be polygon geometry.
  • A projected analysis CRS is required for distance operations.
  • Roads are prepared within the envelope of the study boundary expanded by 30 km; they are not clipped to the study polygon itself.
  • Coverage is based on the largest overlap with one individual buffer, not the union of all overlaps.
  • Cancellation is cooperative and occurs at safe processing checkpoints.
