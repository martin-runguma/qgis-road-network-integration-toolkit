Integration methods

Three ways to combine complementary road networks

Each method preserves the priority network as the reference, but treats eligible secondary roads differently.

Method 01

Direct Merge

Retains all priority roads and appends eligible secondary roads without trimming or snapping. Original secondary geometries are preserved.

Best suited to: fast integration where retaining original geometry is important.

Method 02

Clip + Snap

Subtracts the dissolved priority-buffer mask, creates regularly spaced anchors on priority roads, and snaps remaining line-part endpoints to nearby anchors.

Best suited to: trimming covered portions and connecting nearby endpoints.

Method 03

Targeted Snap

Uses overlap context to create local buffers and local snap targets, trying nearby midpoints and endpoints before global targets.

Best suited to: locally guided connections around overlap areas.
